## Collection

### Facebook Reels

Facebook Reels enables people, creators and businesses to create entertaining short-form videos and share them with their followers. Reels ads provide an immersive, built-for-mobile opportunity to reach audiences seeking entertainment through their interests.
You can use the collection format in Facebook Reels to make it easier for people to discover, browse and purchase products and services from their mobile device in a visual and immersive way.
Collection ads in Facebook Reels can only be created when you select [Advantage+ catalog](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/397103717129942) as your creative source.

### Design Recommendations

- Image Type: JPG or PNG <sup class="_7hhf _5je4"><img class="_2qnn _astl img" src="http://static.xx.fbcdn.net/mci_ab/public/cms/?ab_b=e&amp;ab_page=CMS&amp;ab_entry=2681401055422635&amp;version=1780996758" alt="" loading="eager" style=""></sup>
- Video File Type: MP4, MOV or GIF <sup class="_7hhf _5je4"><img class="_2qnn _astl img" src="http://static.xx.fbcdn.net/mci_ab/public/cms/?ab_b=e&amp;ab_page=CMS&amp;ab_entry=2681401055422635&amp;version=1780996758" alt="" loading="eager" style=""></sup>
- Ratio: 9:16 to 1:1
- Resolution: At least 1080 x 1080 pixels <sup class="_7hhf _5je4"><img class="_2qnn _astl img" src="http://static.xx.fbcdn.net/mci_ab/public/cms/?ab_b=e&amp;ab_page=CMS&amp;ab_entry=2681401055422635&amp;version=1780996758" alt="" loading="eager" style=""></sup>

Consider leaving roughly 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom, and 6% on each side of your asset free from text, logos or other key creative elements to avoid covering them with the profile icon or a call to action. To learn more, visit the [guidance on text overlays and safe zones.](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490)

### Text Recommendations

- Primary Text: 72 characters <sup class="_7hhf _5je4"><img class="_2qnn _astl img" src="http://static.xx.fbcdn.net/mci_ab/public/cms/?ab_b=e&amp;ab_page=CMS&amp;ab_entry=2681401055422635&amp;version=1780996758" alt="" loading="eager" style=""></sup>

- Landing Page URL: Required

### Technical Requirements

- Instant Experience: Required
- Image Maximum File Size: 30MB
- Video Maximum File Size: 4GB

## Collection

### Facebook Feed

Facebook Feed is the constantly updating list of status updates, photos, videos and more in the middle of the Facebook home page. Feed includes updates and posts from friends, Pages, Groups and advertisers.
You can use the collection format in the Facebook Feed to make it easier for people to discover, browse and purchase products and services from their mobile device in a visual and immersive way.
A collection ad in Facebook Feed includes a cover image or video followed by 3 product images. When someone taps on a collection ad, they see an [Instant Experience](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/183469315334462)—a full-screen landing page that drives engagement and nurtures interest and intent. When you create your Instant Experience, you can use the Storefront, Lookbook or Customer Acquisition templates, or build a custom Instant Experience.

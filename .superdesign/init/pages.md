# Help page dependency discovery

All local imports traced recursively; duplicate nodes shown once per page. Non-UI logic belongs to discovery, not design upload context. The target is the existing size guide; related care and materials routes establish help-page conventions. Root shell is documented separately in layouts.md.

## Current implementation: `src/app/handlehjelp/storrelsesguide/page.mdx`

Approved on 2026-09-07 with one correction: TechDown headings and table columns are only Middels, Stor and Større. EU equivalents remain in the relevant FAQ answer.

The route now owns one MDX article. Its direct rendering dependencies are `page.module.css`, the server-only `components/MeasurementTable.tsx` adapter and the existing `LazyFeaturedProductCarousel.tsx` subtree documented below. `layout.tsx` owns metadata, breadcrumbs and `components/SizeGuideJsonLd.tsx`; JSON-LD consumes `utils/techDownFaq.ts`. The old presentational size-guide components have been removed after checking consumers. Shared measurement and size-advice utilities remain in use by product dialogs and assistants.
